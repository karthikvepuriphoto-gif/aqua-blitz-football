HOW TO PLAY

🏈 Goal: score more points than the other team. Touchdown = 6, field goal = 3. You have 4 tries (downs) to move the ball 10 yards. The yellow line is the first-down line.

🧠 Strategy: before every play you pick a play. Read the defense! The coach tip tells you what they are doing. Blitz? Throw quick. Stacking the box? Throw deep. If you keep running the same play, the defense starts to catch on.

🎯 QB: tap a receiver's letter (or press 1–5) to throw. Running plays hand the ball to the RB, and you control him.

💨 RB: follow your blockers. Space/JUKE makes a defender miss. Hold Shift/SPRINT to go fast.

🙌 WR: run your route (dotted line), or let go of the stick and you'll run it on autopilot. When you're open the QB throws. Run under the ring where the ball lands! E/CALL asks for the ball, Space/DIVE dives for it.

🛡️ Defense: chase the ball carrier and touch him to tackle. Space/DIVE is a diving tackle. E/SWITCH jumps to the defender closest to the ball. Get near the ball when it's thrown for an interception!

⌨️ Move with Arrows or WASD. Enter or Space hikes the ball. On a phone or tablet, use the joystick and buttons.
